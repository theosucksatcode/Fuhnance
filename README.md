# Fuhnance

Budgeting, but make it finance bro.

## What Fuhnance is

You know the type. Talks a big game about money, dresses the part, always sounds sure of themselves. Somehow still has zero emergency fund and a credit card that's basically a second rent payment.

Fuhnance takes the confidence and drops the nonsense. It's built for people who just want a budget that's actually correct, not another spreadsheet with two "Banking Fees" rows and R100 left over after everything already ate the plate.

We're not selling loans. We're not pushing credit. We're not another app quietly nudging you toward buy now pay later. We're here to help you build a budget that works.

## Our mascot: Cash

A golden retriever in a quarter-zip and sunglasses. All the confidence of a person who definitely knows what they're doing with money, pointed in a useful direction for once.

## What we actually believe

Savings isn't a leftover. It's not what's left after Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, F1 TV, and vape money get their cut. It gets paid first, like a bill you owe yourself.

A budget built correctly is worth more than a budget built fast. We walk you through it step by step: income, fixed costs, debt, savings, then the fun stuff. Not a blank spreadsheet and good luck.

Your budget shouldn't need to be rebuilt every month. Real life doesn't reset on the 1st, neither should your budget. Build it once, adjust it when something actually changes.

No loan pushing, ever. We make money by helping you keep yours, not by selling you someone else's.

## Where Fuhnance is going

Right now it's about getting the budget process right. A guided experience that turns "I kind of just guessed" into something you can actually trust.

Later on: your bank feed shows up in the app, no manual entry. When a transaction description doesn't make sense, you tell it what it was in plain English and it learns from there. It protects your savings and shows you, with real numbers, what happens if you put a bit extra toward that credit card this month instead of letting it sit.

Budgeting for people who've never opened a trading app in their life and don't need to.

Fuhnance. Not financial advice. Just y'know. Actually good advice.
